Four checkpoints. A clear scope before build. A runbook you can keep.

14day target window

  1. 01

    We dig into your workflow, the data you touch, and the friction you feel. Output: a one-page scope and a clear yes/no on whether automation is the right move.

04FAQ

Common questions, answered straight.

Before you brief us, here are the operating principles.

Q.01Do you only work with Indonesian clients?

No. We work in English and Bahasa Indonesia. Clients across Southeast Asia and abroad use our blueprints. Currency on this page is in IDR for clarity; we invoice in USD or IDR based on your preference.

Q.02What if my automation needs grow over time?

That's normal. Subscription tier can scale up pipelines, add integrations, and increase VPS resources as you go. Pricing scales linearly with usage, never surprise-jumps.

Q.03What stack do you use?

Pragmatically. We pick tools that fit the job — n8n, Python, OpenRouter, VPS hosts (Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Vultr), Discord, Meta Graph API, WhatsApp Business. We avoid vendor lock-in unless it actively helps.

Q.04How is my data handled?

We sign DPAs, never train on your data, and isolate each client's automation on dedicated VPS resources. Off-boarding includes a clean export and credential rotation.
