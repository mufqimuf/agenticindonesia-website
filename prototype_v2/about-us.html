About us

that actually ships.

Indonesian team. Global standards. Local market understanding.

Our Story

We started agenticindonesia because we saw a gap: businesses in Indonesia need automation, but most solutions are either too expensive, too generic, or require expertise that local teams don't have.

We're a distributed team of developers, designers, and automation specialists who understand the Indonesian market. We speak your language, respect your timezone, and build solutions that fit your budget.

No fluff. No vendor lock-in. Just practical automation that moves your business forward.

How we work

Our principles guide every project we take on.

  • 01

    Async-first

    No meeting marathons. We communicate clearly through docs, threads, and short syncs. Your time is valuable.

  • 02

    Transparent pricing

    No hidden fees. No surprise invoices. You know exactly what you're paying for before we start.

  • 03

    Shipped, not stalled

    We measure success by what runs in production, not by hours logged. If it's not working, we fix it.

  • 04

    Local context

    We understand Indonesian business culture, payment systems, and market dynamics. No cultural translation needed.
